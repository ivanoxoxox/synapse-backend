FAQ

Got questions?

Everything you need to know about Synapse events. Can't find the answer? Reach out to us directly.

Do I need an account to browse events? ⌄
You can browse all events without an account. However, you'll need to create a free account to register for any event and receive your QR ticket.
Are the events really free? ⌄
Yes! All Synapse events are completely free to attend. We are community-funded and believe in open access to knowledge.
How do I use my QR ticket? ⌄
After registering, your QR ticket appears instantly in your My Tickets page. Show it at the venue entrance for scanning and check-in.
Can I register for multiple events? ⌄
Absolutely! One account lets you register for as many Synapse events as you like. All your tickets are stored in one place.
What if I can no longer attend? ⌄
We understand things come up. Simply contact our team and we'll handle your cancellation. We appreciate a heads-up so we can offer your spot to someone on the waitlist.
